Every morning

Check supplies out. Check them back in.

  • Start a project for the client and the day, or open today's.
  • Scan each barcode with the phone's camera, or pick an item that has none, and say how many.
  • Back from the job, switch to Return and scan what's unused. The project shows taken, returned, used and the charge.
  • Storage counts follow every scan, and everyone's phone shows the changes within seconds.

Company equipment

Know where every ladder and vacuum is.

  • Mark ladders, vacuums and cords as company equipment, with what each is worth.
  • They go out on a job's project and come back with a scan, but never appear on the client's bill.
  • One list shows what's still out, on which job, who took it and when.
  • Something lost or broken? Charge the client for it in one step, or don't.

Not every grab is a job

A box of gloves for the van? Quick take.

  • Scan what you're taking. No project needed: it goes on General Use (no job) and storage counts drop.
  • Bring something back and scan it. It returns to wherever it's out.
  • Later find out it was for a client? Move the line to their project and bill it.

After the hardware store

Photograph the receipt. Every line lands on the right job.

  • Snap the receipt and the lines are read for you, matched to your inventory where they can be.
  • Check each line, then assign it to a client's project or to general inventory.
  • If a price changed, choose the receipt's price or keep the client's. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
