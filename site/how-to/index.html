How to use Supply Checkout

Short lessons, one job each. Every step has a picture of the screen, with what to tap outlined. Do them in order, or jump to the one you need. Setting up a new team? Start with lessons 5 and 6.

  1. 1 Check supplies out to a job and back in Start a project, scan what you take, scan back what's left. 8 steps, about 5 minutes.
  2. 2 Take supplies without a job Quick take for the van, bring it back, or bill a client later. 6 steps, about 3 minutes.
  3. 3 Take company equipment to a job and bring it back Ladders, vacuums and cords: out on a job, back, or lost or broken. 7 steps, about 5 minutes.
  4. 4 Read a receipt Photograph it, check each line, and put it on the right job or in storage. 6 steps, about 5 minutes.
  5. 5 Set up your inventory Add items one at a time or import a spreadsheet, and see what's running low. For owners. 8 steps, about 5 minutes.
  6. 6 Invite your crew and choose what they can do Invite people by email as owners, contributors or viewers. For owners. 5 steps, about 3 minutes.

Want to try it without signing in? Open the demo: it has some projects and inventory to practice on, and nothing you do there is saved.
