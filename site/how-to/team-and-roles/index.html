How-to › Lesson 6

Invite your crew and choose what they can do

About this lesson

What you'll do
Invite your crew by email so they see the team's projects and inventory, and choose what each person can do: owners run the team and its billing, contributors do the daily work, and viewers can look but not change anything.
How long it takes
About 3 minutes.
You need
A laptop or phone, signed in as the team's owner, and each person's email address.

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Open Members

    In the bar under the header, tap Members. Only the team's owners see it.

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Invite someone

    Under Invite someone, type their email in Email and pick a Role: Contributor for crew who check supplies out and back in, Viewer for a bookkeeper who only needs to look, Owner for someone who runs the team with you. Tap Send invite.

  3. Step 3 of 5

    It waits for them to join

    They get an email with a link that works once and lasts 7 days. Until they join, the invite is listed here. Didn't arrive? Tap Resend. Sent to the wrong person? Tap Revoke.

  4. Step 4 of 5

    See who's on the team

    Under Members, each person has their role. To change one, pick a new role in their list. To take someone off the team, tap Remove, then tap again to be sure. A team always keeps at least one owner.

  5. Step 5 of 5

    They tap the link and join

    Your crew member opens the email on their phone, taps the link, and signs in or signs up. Then they tap Join, and the team's projects are right there.

Stuck?

  • The email didn't arrive. Ask them to check their spam folder, then tap Resend. Check the address is right: if it isn't, Revoke the invite and send a new one.
  • The link says it has expired or was used. Each link works once, for 7 days. Tap Resend to send a new one.
  • Someone sees projects but can't change anything. They're a viewer. Change their role to Contributor in Members.
