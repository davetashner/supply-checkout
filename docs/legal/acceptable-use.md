## Use the Service for its purpose

Supply Checkout is for tracking supplies, inventory and receipts for your business. Don't use it to store or share content unrelated to that.

## Don't

1. **Break the law.** This includes fraud, false billing, tax evasion, and handling stolen goods.
2. **Upload data you have no right to.** Only add client and supplier information you are allowed to use.
3. **Store sensitive personal data** the Service isn't built for, such as payment card numbers, bank account numbers, government ID numbers, health information, or passwords. Receipt photos showing only the last four digits of a card are fine.
4. **Upload harmful content**, such as malware, or content that is illegal, threatening, harassing or sexually explicit.
5. **Get into what isn't yours.** Don't try to access other teams' data, other people's accounts, or our systems beyond what the app lets you do.
6. **Probe or attack the Service.** Don't scan for weaknesses, test security, or get around limits (roles, receipt limits, rate limits) without our written permission. To report a security problem, see [Reporting problems](#reporting-problems).
7. **Overload the Service.** Don't send automated traffic, scrape it, or use it in a way that harms its performance for others. Normal use of the app and any API we publish is fine.
8. **Misuse receipt reading.** Only send real receipts and supply documents. Don't use it to process unrelated images or to try to make the AI model do something other than read receipts.
9. **Share or resell accounts.** Each seat is for one person. Don't share sign-ins, and don't resell or rent the Service to others without a written agreement with us.
10. **Copy the Service.** Don't reverse engineer it, except where the law allows, or use it to build a competing product.
11. **Pretend to be someone else**, or misrepresent your connection to a business.

## What we do if this policy is broken

- We may remove content, limit features, or suspend the accounts involved, as the Terms of Service describe.
- Where we reasonably can, we warn the owner first and give time to fix the problem.
- We act without warning when there is a risk to the Service, to other customers, or of legal harm.
- We may report illegal activity to the authorities.
